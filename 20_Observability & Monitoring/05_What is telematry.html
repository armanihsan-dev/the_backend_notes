WHAT IS TELEMETRY?

THE FOUNDATION OF OBSERVABILITY

TELEMETRY EXPLAINED SIMPLY

Telemetry comes from Greek roots:

Tele

Remote

Metron

Measure

The Simple Definition

Telemetry is the automatic collection and transmission of data from remote sources to a central system for monitoring and analysis.

Literally: "Measuring from a distance"

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES OF TELEMETRY

🚗 In Cars

Modern cars constantly send data to manufacturers:

  • Engine performance
  • Battery status
  • Tire pressure
  • Location (in some cases)
  • Error codes
Real Example

When your "Check Engine" light comes on, the dealer plugs in a device and reads the telemetry to see what happened. The car has been collecting data the entire time.

🚀 In Space Exploration

NASA receives telemetry from spacecraft millions of miles away:

  • Temperature readings
  • Fuel levels
  • System health
  • Position and velocity

🏥 In Healthcare

Hospital monitors send patient telemetry:

  • Heart rate
  • Blood pressure
  • Oxygen levels
  • Temperature

HOW TELEMETRY RELATES TO OUR TOPIC

Telemetry is the OVERARCHING CONCEPT. The three pillars (metrics, logs, traces) are all types of telemetry data.

Think of it This Way

TELEMETRY (The umbrella term)
    ├── 📊 METRICS (Numerical measurements)
    ├── 🪵 LOGS (Event records)
    └── 🧵 TRACES (Request journeys)
The Relationship Explained

Telemetry = All data automatically collected from your production systems

Observability = The ability to understand your system by analyzing that telemetry

Or Even Simpler

Telemetry

The data you collect

Observability

What you can do with that data

A PRACTICAL ANALOGY

🏥 Human Health Version

Concept Analogy
Telemetry All the data from your hospital monitors: heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, oxygen levels, EKG readings
Metrics Your heart rate (80 bpm) and temperature (98.6°F) - numbers over time
Logs Nurse's notes: "Patient complained of headache at 2:15 PM, given medication at 2:17 PM"
Traces The complete story of how a medication moved from prescription to administration to effect
Observability The doctor's ability to look at all this data and understand exactly what's wrong with you

💻 Production System Version

Concept Example
Telemetry Everything your system automatically sends out: metrics, logs, traces
Metrics "CPU is at 80%, requests per second = 1000, error rate = 5%"
Logs "2024-01-15 10:30:23 ERROR: Database connection timeout for user 12345"
Traces "Request took 5 seconds: 4.5 seconds spent in database, 0.5 seconds in app logic"
Observability Your ability to combine all this to know: "The database is slow because of a missing index, affecting users with large carts"

WHY THIS DISTINCTION MATTERS

🎯 Telemetry is the RAW MATERIAL

You must first collect telemetry before you can have observability:

  • Install agents to collect metrics
  • Set up logging frameworks
  • Implement tracing libraries
  • Send all this data to central storage

🔍 Observability is the CAPABILITY

Once you have telemetry, observability is what you can achieve:

  • Ask arbitrary questions about system behavior
  • Debug without guessing
  • Understand unknown-unknowns
💡 Key Insight

You can't have observability without telemetry, just like you can't have a diagnosis without medical tests. The more telemetry you have (and the better it's structured), the higher your observability on the spectrum.

THE SPECTRUM CONNECTS TO TELEMETRY

Remember "Observability is a spectrum"? That spectrum is really about:

How much useful telemetry do you have, and how well can you use it?

Observability Level Telemetry You Have
Level 0 (Blind) No telemetry
Level 1 (Uptime only) Basic metrics only
Level 2 (Metrics + Dashboards) Good metrics
Level 3 (+ Structured Logs) Metrics + Logs
Level 4 (+ Tracing) Metrics + Logs + Traces
Level 5 (Full observability) All telemetry, perfectly correlated

A CONCRETE EXAMPLE

🛒 E-commerce Site Problem

Without Telemetry

Customer: "Your site is slow"

You: "Is it? I don't know. Let me check manually..."

With Basic Telemetry (Metrics Only)

Dashboard shows: Response time increased from 200ms to 5 seconds

You know: Something is slow

You don't know: What or why

With Full Telemetry (Metrics + Logs + Traces)

Metrics: Checkout endpoint latency spike

Traces: Database query taking 4.5 seconds

Logs: "Full table scan on orders table for user with 10,000 orders"

You know: Missing index on user_id column

You fix: Add index, problem solved ✅
