Complete Guide: All 8 Razorpay Payment Methods + Lemon Squeezy Comparison

1. Payment Links

The Digital Price Tag

Think of it as: A "Buy Now" URL you can paste anywhere — like a digital price tag you can text, email, or share on social media.

  • One product/amount = One unique link
  • No website needed — works via any messaging app
  • Perfect for: "Just send me a link to pay!" situations

Best For:

  • Freelancers sending "pay me" links
  • Quick invoices for one-off services
  • Event tickets sent via WhatsApp

2. Payment Pages

Your Instant Online Store

Think of it as: A ready-to-use product page hosted by Razorpay — like creating an Amazon listing without needing your own website.

  • Full product page with images, description, options
  • Your own custom URL (yourbrand.razorpay.com/your-product)
  • Customer browses → Selects options → Pays all in one place

Best For:

  • Selling single products without a full e-commerce site
  • Donation/campaign pages
  • Digital products (ebooks, templates, courses)

3. Payment Buttons

Copy-Paste Checkout

Think of it as: A "Pay Now" button you can embed anywhere — like adding a checkout button to your blog or simple website with just copy-paste.

  • No coding needed — paste the HTML snippet
  • Click opens secure checkout in popup
  • Customer never leaves your page

Best For:

  • Bloggers accepting donations/tips
  • Simple websites without shopping carts
  • Non-technical users who need payments fast

4. Checkout Integration

The Professional Shopping Cart

Think of it as: A custom-branded checkout built into your website/app — like Amazon's checkout but for your business.

  • Seamless experience — customer stays on your site
  • Full control over design and flow
  • Handles carts with multiple items

Best For:

  • Full e-commerce stores
  • SaaS platforms with in-app purchases
  • Businesses wanting branded checkout experience

5. QR Codes

The Tap-and-Pay for Physical World

Think of it as: A scannable price tag — turn any counter, bill, or poster into a payment point.

  • Static QR: Fixed amount (like ₹100 for a t-shirt)
  • Dynamic QR: Enter amount then pay (like restaurant bill)
  • Customer scans → Pays via UPI app → Instant confirmation

Best For:

  • Retail stores & restaurants
  • Bill payments at service centers
  • Events and conference registrations

6. Subscriptions

Set It and Forget It Payments

Think of it as: Auto-renewing memberships — like Netflix billing you every month without asking.

  • Customer pays once → gets charged automatically each period
  • Handles upgrades, downgrades, pauses, cancellations
  • Smart retry logic for failed payments

Best For:

  • SaaS & software companies
  • Membership sites (premium content)
  • Subscription boxes/services

7. Invoices

The Professional Bill

Think of it as: A digital bill with a "Pay Now" button — like sending a paper invoice, but clickable and trackable.

  • Formal bill with your branding, terms, due date
  • Line items, taxes, discounts clearly shown
  • Track who paid, who didn't, send reminders

Best For:

  • B2B businesses
  • Agencies, consultants, freelancers
  • Service businesses with custom pricing

8. Smart Collect

Your Customer's Personal Bank Account

Think of it as: Giving each customer their own payment address — like having a unique bank account number for every order.

  • Each customer gets unique:
    • Virtual bank account number (for NEFT/IMPS)
    • UPI ID (for instant payments)
  • No cards needed — works with any bank account
  • Auto-matches payments to right orders

Best For:

  • Large B2B transactions
  • Customers who prefer bank transfers
  • High-value payments (education, real estate)
