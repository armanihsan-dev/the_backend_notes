Lemon Squeezy Payment States

In Lemon Squeezy, payments go through several states that represent different stages of the transaction lifecycle. Here are the primary payment states you'll encounter:

Order-Level Payment States

These are the main states displayed in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard:

  • ✅Paid

    Payment successfully completed

    Funds have been captured

    Most common successful state

  • ⏳Pending

    Payment initiated but not yet confirmed

    Common with bank transfers, some card payments

    Awaiting settlement/confirmation

  • ↩️Refunded

    Full or partial refund issued to customer

    Can be partial or full refunds

  • ❌Cancelled

    Payment was cancelled before completion

    Can be cancelled by customer or merchant

    No funds captured

Subscription-Specific States

  • 🧪On Trial

    Customer in free trial period

    Payment not yet collected

  • ⚠️Past Due

    Payment attempt failed

    Subscription still active (retry period)

  • 💳Unpaid

    Payment failed and not recovered

    Subscription may be suspended/ended

Other Transaction States

  • 🚫Voided

    Authorization was voided before settlement

    No funds transferred

  • ⚠️Disputed/Chargeback

    Customer disputed the charge

    Funds temporarily held during investigation

  • 📝Created

    Order/checkout session created

    Payment not yet attempted

    Initial state in the workflow

Payment Processing Flow

Typical progression:

Created → Pending → Paid
        ↘
         Cancelled/Voided
        ↘
         Failed → (Retry) → Unpaid

API & Webhook States

When working with the API or webhooks, you might also see:

  • payment_intent.created - Initial payment intent
  • payment_intent.succeeded - Payment completed
  • payment_intent.payment_failed - Payment failed
  • invoice.paid - Subscription invoice paid
  • invoice.payment_failed - Subscription payment failed

Important Notes:

  • "Paid" is the ultimate successful state
  • "Pending" can last from minutes to several business days
  • "Refunded" orders can still appear in analytics but marked as refunded
  • Subscription states differ from one-time payment states
  • Most states are visible in both dashboard and via API

You can filter orders by these states in your Lemon Squeezy dashboard, and webhooks will notify you of state changes for automated processing.
