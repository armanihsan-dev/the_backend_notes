What Are Orders in Lemon Squeezy?

Orders are the core transaction records in Lemon Squeezy that represent a purchase or subscription. Each order contains:

Key Components of an Order:

  • Customer Information - Name, email, billing details
  • Product/Variant Details - What was purchased
  • Payment Information - Amount, currency, payment method
  • Status - Payment state (paid, pending, refunded, etc.)
  • License Keys - For digital products (if applicable)
  • Tax & Compliance Data - VAT, tax calculations

Order Types:

  • One-time purchases - Single payment for a product
  • Subscription orders - Initial payment + recurring charges
  • Renewal orders - Each subscription renewal creates a new order
  • Refund orders - Records of refunds issued

Order Flow Example:

Checkout Created → Order Created → Payment Processed → Order Completed
