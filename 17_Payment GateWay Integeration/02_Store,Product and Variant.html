Understanding the Lemon Squeezy Hierarchy

Understanding the hierarchy of Store → Product → Variants is crucial for using Lemon Squeezy effectively. Let me break it down with clear analogies and examples.

The Hierarchy (Big Picture)

Think of it like a physical store setup:

  • Store = Your entire shop/business
  • Product = A type of item you sell (e.g., "T-Shirt")
  • Variant = Specific version of that item (e.g., "T-Shirt - Size Large, Blue")

1. Store 🏪

What it is: Your entire Lemon Squeezy account/space. Everything exists under one store.

Key characteristics:

  • One account = One store (though Lemon Squeezy now allows multiple stores per account)
  • Contains all your products, customers, and orders
  • Has your store URL (yourstore.lemonsqueezy.com)
  • Where you configure payments, taxes, and general settings

Example:

  • Store Name: "Digital Yoga Academy"
  • Store URL: digitalyoga.lemonsqueezy.com
  • This store sells: yoga courses, meditation guides, and planners

2. Product 📦

What it is: A sellable item in your store. Each product represents a distinct offering.

Key characteristics:

  • Has a name, description, and base price
  • Can have multiple variants (or just one if it's simple)
  • Has its own product page

Examples:

  • "Beginner Yoga Course" - A video course
  • "Meditation Journal PDF" - A digital download
  • "Yoga Planner Template" - A Notion template

Real Lemon Squeezy example:


Product:
  Name: "Notion Productivity System"
  Description: "A complete Notion template for managing tasks..."
  Base Price: $49
  Product Type: Digital Download

3. Variant 🔄

What it is: A specific version of a product with its own price, files, or attributes.

Key characteristics:

  • Belongs to a product
  • Can differ in: price, files, license keys, or attributes (like size, duration, tier)
  • Customers select a variant at checkout

Why use variants? Instead of creating separate products for similar items, group them as variants.

Node.js Course Example

PRODUCT: "Master Node.js - Complete Developer Course"

Description: A comprehensive Node.js course with projects, exercises, and community access.

VARIANTS = Different Access Plans:

Variant 1: "Basic Plan"

  • Price: $97
  • Access Duration: 1 year
  • Includes: Course videos, basic exercises, Discord access
  • Files: Course videos ZIP, exercise files

Variant 2: "Pro Plan"

  • Price: $197
  • Access Duration: 2 years
  • Includes: Everything in Basic + code reviews, project feedback, priority support
  • Files: Course videos ZIP, exercise files, bonus workshops

Variant 3: "Lifetime Plan"

  • Price: $497
  • Access Duration: Lifetime (forever)
  • Includes: Everything in Pro + all future updates, private mastermind
  • Files: Course videos ZIP, exercise files, bonus workshops, future updates

How This Looks in Lemon Squeezy Dashboard:

Creating the Product:

  • Go to Products → New Product
  • Name: "Master Node.js - Complete Developer Course"
  • Description: (Your course description)
  • Price: $97 (this becomes your base/starting price)

Adding Variants:

  • Click "Add variant" on the product page
  • Variant 1 Name: "Basic - 1 Year Access"
  • Price: $97
  • Description: "1 year access to all course materials..."
  • Variant 2 Name: "Pro - 2 Year Access"
  • Price: $197
  • Description: "2 years access with code reviews..."
  • Variant 3 Name: "Lifetime Access"
  • Price: $497
  • Description: "Lifetime access with all future updates..."
