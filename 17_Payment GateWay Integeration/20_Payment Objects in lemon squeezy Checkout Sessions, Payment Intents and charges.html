Similar Concepts in Lemon Squeezy:

1. Checkout Sessions $\to$ Checkouts

  • Lemon Squeezy Equivalent: "Checkouts" (one-time) or "Checkout URLs"
    Functionality similar to **Stripe's Checkout Sessions** for collecting payment
    Can be created via **API** or **dashboard**
    Returns a **checkout URL** to redirect customers

2. Payment Intents $\to$ Orders + Checkouts

  • Lemon Squeezy Equivalent: No direct equivalent, but the combination of:
    **Checkouts** handle the payment collection
    **Orders** represent the finalized transaction
    Unlike Stripe, Lemon Squeezy **doesn't expose a separate "intent" object** - the flow is more streamlined

3. Charges $\to$ Order Items + Invoices

  • Lemon Squeezy Equivalent: Payment data is split between:
    **Order Items:** Individual products/services purchased
    **Invoices:** Payment records (available in Lemon Squeezy's interface)
    **Orders:** The overall transaction containing order items

Key Differences in Architecture:

  • Simpler Model
    Lemon Squeezy combines what would be multiple Stripe objects into **fewer entities**
  • Product-Centric
    Built around **digital products/subscriptions** rather than general payments
  • Less Granular Control
    While Stripe exposes every step, Lemon Squeezy **abstracts more of the payment flow**
  • Built-in Tax/VAT
    Handled **automatically** vs. Stripe where you implement it

What's Missing:

  • No direct equivalent to **PaymentMethods** object (payment methods are associated with customers)
  • No **SetupIntents** for saving payment methods without immediate payment
  • Less flexibility for **custom payment flows**

Workflow Comparison:

Stripe: Checkout Session $\to$ Payment Intent $\to$ Charge

Lemon Squeezy: Checkout $\to$ Order (with order items)
