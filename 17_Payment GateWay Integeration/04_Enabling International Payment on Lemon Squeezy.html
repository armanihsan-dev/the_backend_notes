Lemon Squeezy – International Payments

Notes on how international payments work and how they are enabled in Lemon Squeezy.

How to Enable International Payments on Lemon Squeezy

Lemon Squeezy does not require a manual toggle or setting to enable international payments. Once your store is live, international payments are enabled automatically.

  • Create a Lemon Squeezy account and store
  • Complete business details and identity verification (KYC)
  • Activate your store (go live mode)
  • Set up payouts (bank account or PayPal)
As soon as the store is activated, customers from supported countries can pay without any extra configuration.

“Lemon Squeezy is built to support international payments out of the box”

This statement means that international payment support is a default feature of Lemon Squeezy.

  • No country-specific payment setup required
  • No need to integrate Stripe or PayPal manually
  • Global cards and wallets work automatically
  • Taxes, VAT, and compliance handled by Lemon Squeezy
Lemon Squeezy acts as the Merchant of Record, meaning it legally sells the product on your behalf and handles global payment complexity.

Supported Payment Methods (Global)

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • Apple Pay & Google Pay
  • PayPal
  • Region-specific wallets (based on customer location)

Available payment methods depend on the customer’s country and device.

Key Takeaway

You do not enable international payments manually in Lemon Squeezy. If your store is activated and payouts are configured, you are already accepting payments globally.
