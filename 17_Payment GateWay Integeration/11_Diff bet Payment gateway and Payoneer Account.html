🆚 Payment Gateway vs. Global Payout Network

Feature 🍋 Lemon Squeezy (Payment Gateway / Merchant of Record) 🌎 Payoneer (Global Payout / Receiving Account)
Primary Function Processing payments from customers and handling all sales tax, VAT, and compliance (acting as the **Merchant of Record**). Receiving international payments from companies (like Amazon, Google, or Lemon Squeezy) and facilitating cross-border transfers and payouts to local banks.
Who Uses It? Sellers/Merchants who need to accept payments on their website or digital storefront. Freelancers, businesses, and global workers who need to get paid by clients or platforms in different currencies.
Where Money Comes From The **End Customer** (via credit card, PayPal, etc.). **Companies/Platforms** (like Lemon Squeezy, Upwork, Airbnb, etc.).
Transaction Visibility Sees the customer's full checkout process, including taxes, discounts, and payment failures. Sees the bulk transfer deposited by a platform; does not see the end customer's details.
Core Service E-commerce infrastructure, tax compliance, and payment acceptance. Banking-like services, multi-currency accounts, and funds withdrawal/payout.
Analogy The **Cash Register and Compliance Office** for your store. The **International Bank Account** that receives your business income.

Detailed Roles in the Ecosystem

🍋 1. Lemon Squeezy: The Merchant of Record (MoR)

Lemon Squeezy is much more than just a payment gateway; it is primarily a **Merchant of Record** service focused on selling digital products.

Key Roles of Lemon Squeezy:

  • Payment Processing: It handles the technical side of accepting payments (connecting to Visa/Mastercard, etc.).
  • Tax Compliance: This is their biggest value. They calculate, collect, file, and remit sales tax, VAT, and GST in over 100 countries on your behalf. This is mandatory for international digital sales.
  • Merchant of Record (MoR): When a customer buys something, they are legally buying it **from Lemon Squeezy**, not directly from you. This offloads all complex compliance and liability from your business.
  • Subscription Management: They handle recurring billing, failed payment retries, and customer portals.

**Summary:** Lemon Squeezy is the tool that handles the complex, customer-facing, and legal aspects of your sales transaction.

🌎 2. Payoneer: The Global Payout Account

Payoneer is a financial services company that operates as a **cross-border payment platform**. It acts like a virtual bank account designed for international business.

Key Roles of Payoneer:

  • Global Receiving Accounts: They provide you with local bank account details (like U.S. ACH, EU SEPA, UK Faster Payments) so that international companies can pay you as if you were a local business, often avoiding high international wire fees.
  • Funds Aggregation: You use it to consolidate earnings from various sources (e.g., you can link your Lemon Squeezy payouts, Amazon earnings, and freelance client payments to your Payoneer balance).
  • Withdrawal: You can hold funds in multiple currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) or withdraw them to your local bank account in your home currency at competitive exchange rates.
  • Payoneer Card: They often offer a prepaid debit card for spending your balance globally.

**Summary:** Payoneer is the tool that helps you **receive** the money generated from multiple international sources (including Lemon Squeezy) and move it efficiently into your local banking system.

🔄 The Relationship (How They Work Together)

It's common for a seller to use **both** services simultaneously:

  1. Sale: A customer buys a digital product through your website, and **Lemon Squeezy** processes the payment, collects the VAT, and manages the transaction.
  2. Payout: At the end of the month, Lemon Squeezy bundles your earnings and sends a single, large payout (minus their fee) to your preferred method, which can be your local bank or, often more efficiently, your **Payoneer USD or EUR receiving account**.
  3. Withdrawal: You then use **Payoneer** to convert the funds and transfer them to your personal bank account.

**In essence, Lemon Squeezy is the front end (the sales desk and compliance office), and Payoneer is the back end (the global treasury and cash withdrawal system).**
